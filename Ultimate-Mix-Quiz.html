01Executive summary

A ready-to-adapt proposal for a national-level, individual online quiz organised by Catalyst Crew / Catalyst Crew Dev Community.

The Ultimate Mix Quiz is a single-round online quiz hosted on Unstop. It combines technology and computer fundamentals, logical reasoning, riddles, fun trivia, everyday science and general awareness in one assessment. The intended audience includes college students from IITs, NITs, engineering colleges, BCA/BSc programmes, business programmes and other eligible backgrounds.

Positioning statement: “You don’t need to be a coding expert. Bring your curiosity, logic and quick thinking.”

Inclusive

Questions accessible to both technical and non-technical participants.

Competitive

Clear scoring, a timed attempt and a transparent leaderboard policy.

Affordable

Proposed ₹9 entry fee, subject to platform and payment verification.

Objectives

  • Encourage cross-college participation across India.
  • Test practical knowledge, reasoning and lateral thinking.
  • Make quizzes fun without making the paper trivial or overly difficult.
  • Increase awareness of Catalyst Crew and its student community.
  • Create a repeatable format for future online quiz editions.

03Competition format

Single-round model

All participants attempt the same mixed quiz. No separate coding round, team round or presentation.

Assessment type

Objective, single-answer multiple-choice questions with four options per question.

Participation

One individual attempt per participant, only if supported by the final platform configuration.

Accessibility

No advanced programming prerequisite. Basic computer awareness and reasoning are enough to participate.

Recommended difficulty distribution

Easy15 questions · 50%
Moderate12 questions · 40%

Question-writing standards

  • One defensible correct answer for each single-answer question.
  • Four distinct options; distractors should be plausible, not silly or misleading.
  • Keep wording concise and avoid ambiguous riddles with multiple valid interpretations.
  • Do not require obscure facts, paid tools, specialist coursework or advanced algorithms.
  • For current affairs, specify a reference cutoff date and verify every answer.
  • Do not include a question whose answer is revealed by another question.
  • Keep the language consistent across the whole paper; simple English is recommended for national reach.

06Scoring, ranking & tie-break policy

+4

For each correct answer

−1

For each incorrect answer

0

For each unattempted question

Under the proposed scoring system, the maximum score is 30 × 4 = 120 points. A wrong answer incurs a one-point penalty, while leaving a question blank earns zero.

Recommended tie-break order

  1. Higher total score.
  2. Higher number of correct answers.
  3. Shorter completion time, but only if the platform exposes a reliable and comparable time field.
  4. If still tied, use a pre-announced tie-break question or a documented organiser review method that is fair to all tied participants.
Platform dependency: Verify whether Unstop supports negative marking, the desired timer, one-attempt enforcement, answer shuffling and exportable time data in the specific quiz configuration. If a setting is unavailable, update the rules before registration opens; never announce a scoring rule the platform cannot enforce.

Result validation

  • Export or review the official score report where available.
  • Check for duplicate entries, incomplete attempts and obvious data anomalies.
  • Apply the published tie-break order consistently.
  • Have a second organiser review the final top positions.
  • Record the final result approval date and retain an internal audit copy.

07Participant rules & integrity policy

  1. Participation is individual. Collaboration or answer-sharing during the live quiz is not allowed.
  2. Use accurate registration information and the account associated with your entry.
  3. Attempt the quiz only within the official schedule and follow the platform's instructions.
  4. Do not share active quiz questions, screenshots, answers or hints with other participants.
  5. Impersonation, unauthorised assistance, duplicate accounts intended to gain an advantage and exploitation of platform bugs are prohibited.
  6. Participants should arrange a stable internet connection and compatible device before starting.
  7. Participants are responsible for reading the instructions before launching an attempt.
  8. Personal connectivity or device problems do not automatically guarantee extra time or a retake.
  9. Any disqualification must be based on the published policy and available evidence.
  10. Results are subject to the announced verification and tie-break process.
  11. Any query or result concern must be submitted to the official contact within the announced review window.
  12. The platform's applicable terms and final event listing also apply.

Refund and cancellation policy

Publish a clear policy before accepting payment. State how cancellations, event rescheduling, technical failure and eligible refunds will be handled, consistent with Unstop's applicable payment terms and local law. Do not promise that fees are refundable or non-refundable until the actual policy is confirmed.

  • One designated lead owns decisions and participant-facing updates.
  • One person monitors platform announcements and technical issues.

16Frequently asked questions

Is this a hackathon?

No. It is a single-round online quiz. Participants answer MCQs rather than building or submitting a project.

Can IIT and NIT students participate?

The event is intended to be open to eligible students across India, including IITs and NITs. Confirm the final eligibility requirements in the published listing.

Do participants need advanced coding skills?

No. Only part of the quiz is technical. Logic, riddles, fun trivia and general awareness balance the paper.

Is the entry fee definitely ₹9?

₹9 is the proposed participant fee. Confirm that Unstop supports the intended payment setup and clarify applicable charges before publishing.

Are prizes and certificates guaranteed?

No. They must be confirmed and funded or otherwise secured by the organisers before being advertised.

What if a participant has a network problem?

The published support and incident policy will apply. A connectivity issue does not automatically guarantee a retake or extra time.

How will ties be resolved?

Use the pre-announced sequence: score, number of correct answers, reliable completion-time data if available, then a documented final tie-break method.
